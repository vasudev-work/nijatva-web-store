Nijatva Frame

One line puts a browser window inside any page. Below is a live frame on this same page.

How it is done:

<div id="frame" style="height:560px"></div>
<script src="embed.js" data-target="frame" data-url="https://example.com" defer></script>

Or directly, without the script:

<iframe src="embed.html?u=https://example.com" style="width:100%;height:560px;border:0"></iframe>

A site that sets X-Frame-Options or frame-ancestors will refuse to be framed - the frame then says so, and offers to open it in a new tab. That is the site's choice.
